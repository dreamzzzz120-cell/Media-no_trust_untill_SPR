Media Passport Terms

Media Passport provides evidence-based media provenance and trust assessments. Assessments are probabilistic and are not determinations of objective truth, legal ownership, copyright infringement, identity, or regulatory compliance.

Acceptable use

Do not upload unlawful material, malware, credentials, or material you do not have the right to process. Do not attempt to abuse, bypass, or manipulate the service or its evidence pipeline.

Limitations

AI and provenance signals can be wrong or incomplete. A missing provenance credential is not proof of human origin. Risk findings are not legal advice.

Appeals

Users may challenge eligible findings through the appeal workflow and provide supporting evidence.

These terms are a product baseline and should be reviewed and supplemented by qualified counsel before commercial launch.
