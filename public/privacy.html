Media Passport Privacy

Media Passport processes submitted media and associated metadata to provide provenance, evidence, trust, safety, audit, and service functions.

Data minimization

Where practical, the service retains hashes and structured evidence rather than unnecessary copies of source media. Uploaded source media is processed in quarantine and may be deleted after successful verification according to deployment configuration.

Security

Access is protected with authentication, authorization, rate limiting, secure storage, and audit controls. Uploaded media is treated as untrusted input.

Rights

Subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements, users may request access, export, correction, or deletion of applicable personal data.

This is a product baseline and should be reviewed and finalized by qualified privacy counsel before commercial launch.
