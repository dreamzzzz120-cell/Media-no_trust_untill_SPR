# Regulation Packs

Regulation Packs represent specific regulatory or standards requirements as versioned, evidence-evaluable contracts.

Flow:

Regulation Pack -> Requirement -> Control Mapping -> Evidence -> Evaluation -> Audit Package

A pack records jurisdiction, regulation identifier, version, source reference, effective dates and applicability. Requirements remain individually addressable. Control mappings state why a UASA control/law maps to a requirement. Evaluations use only:

- SUPPORTED
- PARTIAL
- UNSUPPORTED
- UNKNOWN
- CONFLICTING
- NOT_APPLICABLE

UNKNOWN is required when coverage/evidence is insufficient. NOT_APPLICABLE requires an applicability basis; it must never be used merely because evidence is missing.

Regulation Packs do not make UASA a regulator, law firm, certification body or accredited auditor. A pack can establish what evidence was observed and how it maps to a requirement. Legal conclusions or certifications require the appropriate external authority.

Initial pack content must be sourced from authoritative regulation/standards material and must preserve jurisdiction, version and effective date. Never silently update a historical pack when its source changes; create a new version and re-evaluate affected requirements.
