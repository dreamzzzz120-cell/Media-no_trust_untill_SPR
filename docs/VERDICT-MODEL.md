# Verdict model

`AI_VERIFIED` requires both provenance verification and a positive synthetic-generation observation.

`HUMAN_ORIGIN_VERIFIED` requires both provenance verification and a positive capture-origin observation.

`UNVERIFIED` means the available evidence cannot establish either origin.

`REVIEW` is reserved for conflicts or inconclusive high-impact evidence.

`BLOCK` is reserved for explicit high-risk manipulation signals or policy decisions. It is not a synonym for "AI".

The model is intentionally asymmetric: uncertainty is not converted into a claim of authenticity.
