# Commercial Exploration Division

Commercial Exploration is governed by the same UASA evidence and authority rules as every other AI activity.

Pipeline:

DISCOVER -> EXTRACT -> VERIFY -> QUALIFY -> OUTREACH -> RESPONSE -> OPPORTUNITY -> CUSTOMER -> EXPANSION

Rules:

- Public or otherwise authorized business information may be observed; collection authority and source must be recorded.
- Verified/observed business facts remain separate from inferred signals.
- No probability of purchase or invented lead score is presented as fact.
- Outreach requires a Corps work order and an evidence-backed contact boundary.
- SUPPRESSED, OPTED_OUT, BOUNCED and UNKNOWN are first-class contact states.
- Automated sending must fail closed unless the applicable contact boundary is ALLOWED and any required approval is evidenced.
- Every outbound message is represented by a content digest and outreach receipt; raw sensitive contact data need not be copied into the evidence graph.
- Delivery, response, opportunity, cost and revenue states are observations, not marketing claims.
- Commercial Black Holes remain UNKNOWN rather than inventing why a prospect stopped responding.
