# Media Passport Policy Baseline

## AI Disclosure Policy
Creators should disclose material AI generation, synthetic people/voices, or material AI editing. Media Passport compares declared use with available evidence and labels mismatches for review.

## Content Policy
Trust scores are evidence summaries. They are not claims that a person, organization, event, or statement is objectively true. High-risk findings can trigger review or suppression recommendations for consuming platforms.

## Copyright / Reuse
Copyright, likeness, trademark and reuse findings are risk indicators only. They do not constitute legal advice or a legal ownership determination. Complaints should be routed through the applicable rights process.

## Privacy / Likeness
Users may report privacy, impersonation, likeness, or sensitive-content concerns. Evidence should be minimized and retained only as necessary for the case and applicable legal obligations.

## Appeals and Corrections
Creators can challenge findings, provide evidence, request reassessment, and request human review. Overrides and false-positive/false-negative outcomes should be retained as evaluation data.

## Deletion and Export
Organizations should be able to request deletion and export of their account data subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and active-case retention requirements.

## Regulatory posture
This product is designed to support AI transparency, provenance, moderation, and evidence workflows. It must not be marketed as a certification of compliance with a specific law without jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## Policy versioning
Policy changes are versioned. Every material trust observation records the policy version used to produce it.
